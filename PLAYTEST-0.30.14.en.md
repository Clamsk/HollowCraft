# HollowCraft 0.30.14: Clamsk Terraria Easter egg

[简体中文](试玩说明-0.30.14.md) | [English](PLAYTEST-0.30.14.en.md)

Adds Zenith and Stardust Wings; see the [Easter egg guide](docs/TERRARIA_EASTER_EGG.en.md). Both bridges are compiled for player testing. Neither game nor a Radiance fight was launched for acceptance.

1. Stop the old build, update/install, then run **Start HollowCraft.cmd**.
2. Press **T** or **/** in gameplay, type **Clamsk**, and press Enter. Leave two backpack slots empty.
3. Click Zenith for one three-sword cycle, or hold to repeat. Screen aiming, ordinary-terrain penetration, colored trails, original component-sword sprites and swing audio are included. Base damage is 190 with a 14% double critical chance; native boss phases and death handling remain active.
4. Equip Stardust Wings in the chest slot. Hold Space airborne for a cumulative three seconds of flight, reset by landing, then hold to slow descent. Torso-bound four-frame flapping, stardust and landing-damage immunity are included.
5. Original assets are imported from the player's Steam Terraria installation; local 1.4.5.8 import completed. The package includes importer code, without game assets. Normal features remain available without Terraria.

Please try single-click/held casts, Radiance phase changes, wing equip/unequip, flight exhaustion/landing reset, chat input recovery and effect cleanup across rooms. Existing boats, shields, totems, building and survival equipment remain included.
